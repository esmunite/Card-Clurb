What is this?

Card Clurb is a bot that hunts down underpriced Pokémon cards on eBay. Every 4 hours it scans listings for graded cards, raw singles, and sealed boxes, scores each one 0–100, and posts the 40 best deals on the homepage. That's the whole thing.

How the scoring works

  • How cheap is it, really — the price vs. what the card actually sells for.
  • Auction heat — ending soon with zero bids is usually where the lowest prices show up.
  • Seller trust — feedback score and ratings. Sketchy sellers don't make the cut.
  • Listing quality — real photos beat stock photos. Every time.

Anything that smells fake — "reprint," "proxy," "custom" — gets tossed, no matter how cheap it looks. Same for beat-up cards hiding behind a mint-condition price.
